Ingredient Sankey & Substitution Mindmap

Two features, specified to the point where implementation needs no further design calls. Every open question from the backlog entries is resolved below, with the reasoning kept so the decision can be revisited rather than re-litigated. Colours are the real Brand tokens; the app is dark-locked, so there is no light variant to design.

Ingredient Sankey

Ingredients on the left flow into the steps that consume them. The edge list already exists in the model — RecipeDirection.ingredients is a [DirectionIngredientRef] carrying a name and an IngredientAmount per step — so this adds no persistence.

Mockup · Chicken stir-fry · 6 ingredients → 4 steps
ribbon · 50% opacity node bar · 100% count unit · not to scale

Resolved decisions

FlowIngredient → consuming step, one stage. Source of truth is RecipeDirection.ingredients. An ingredient referenced by several steps splits across them, proportionally to the per-step amount.
Band heightScaled by quantity, within a unit family only — never across.
Count unitspiece, pinch, dash, bunch, clove, slice, can, asNeeded get a fixed 14 pt band with a dashed leading edge, reading as "present, not to scale." They are never silently scaled against a mass.
ColourIngredientCategory → IngredientColor.swiftUIColor. Ribbons at 50% opacity so crossings stay readable; node bars opaque. Step bars are neutral #3a3a3a — the colour belongs to the ingredient, not the step.
OverflowMore than 8 ingredients: keep the 7 largest, aggregate the rest into a single muted "+N more" band. Do not scroll the chart.
RenderingCanvas with cubic Béziers, control points at the horizontal midpoint (as in the mockup paths). Swift Charts has no Sankey mark — do not wait for one.

Band height formula

// q_base: ml via volumeToMilliliters, or g via weightToGrams
// q_familyMax: the largest q_base among ingredients in the SAME family
h = max(8, 34 * sqrt(q_base / q_familyMax))

Why within-family, not absolute. MeasurementConversionService keeps volume and weight on two separate bases with no density bridge, so "2 cups flour" and "1 lb chicken" genuinely cannot share an axis without per-ingredient density data the app does not have. Comparing like with like is the honest version.

Why the square root. Linear scaling makes a Sankey useless for cooking: stock and water dominate, and the spices that define the dish collapse to hairlines. Damping keeps the ordering truthful while leaving small ingredients legible.

Why the 8 pt floor. Below that a band is neither readable nor tappable.

States — all four must be handled

  • No step links at all. Render nothing; fall back to the flat ingredient list. Not an edge case — RecipeIngestionService is not guaranteed to populate DirectionIngredientRef, so imported recipes will commonly have an empty edge list. Check this first; it is the most likely real-world state.
  • Single step. Degenerate (one fan-in). Fall back to a category bar.
  • Partial links. Some ingredients unreferenced: render them flowing to a muted "unassigned" node rather than dropping them silently.
  • Dynamic Type above XL. Switch to the list form. Do not shrink the chart — labels are already at 12.5 pt here, which is the floor.

Accessibility

  • .accessibilityElement(children: .ignore) on the Canvas, with an .accessibilityLabel summarising ingredient count and step count.
  • Add .accessibilityChartDescriptor so VoiceOver can traverse the flows as data.
  • Never colour-only: every band keeps its text label. The 14 category colours are not all distinguishable at 50% opacity for colour-blind users.

Substitution Mindmap

Tap an ingredient, get its alternatives with ratio and trade-off. SubstitutionEngine.findSubstitutions(for:restrictions:) already returns exactly the fields the node needs. The gap is surfacing, not data — the engine is wired into GuidedShoppingView alone today, so substitutions exist while shopping but not while cooking.

Mockup · two layouts, one data source
Blocking prerequisite — do this first
SubstitutionEngine.findSubstitutions keys knownSubstitutions on the raw lowercased name, with no canonicalisation. So "butter" matches and "Unsalted Butter" silently returns nothing — which is most real recipe text. Route the lookup through IngredientNormalizer.canonicalize. It is roughly a one-line change, it gates this entire feature, and it improves the existing shopping flow on its own.

Resolved decisions

LayoutSame data, two presentations, chosen on horizontalSizeClass: compact gets an inline disclosure, regular gets the radial. The radial is the nicer object; the list is the one that works one-handed at a hob.
Selecting a substituteAppends to CookingLogEntry.substitutionsMade for the current cook. It does not mutate the Recipe.
Saving permanentlyAn explicit secondary action — "Save as variation" — creates a RecipeVariation. IngredientOverride already carries originalIngredient, replacementIngredient, replacementAmount and reason, so this maps 1:1 with no model change.
RatioShow Substitution.ratio verbatim — it is prose ("3/4 cup per 1 cup butter") and must not be parsed optimistically. Offer MeasurementConversionService.scale only for a clean n:m, as an offer. Never auto-rescale.
SurfacesIngredientRow in RecipeDetailView, and CookingModeView. Not the editor — substitution is a cooking-time act.
EmptyEngine returns [] → show no affordance at all. No disclosure arrow that opens onto "none found."

Why annotate the cook rather than edit the recipe. "I'm out of buttermilk" is a one-time event, not a change of mind about the recipe. Rewriting a saved recipe on a single tap is destructive, easy to do by accident, and has no undo once the original amount is gone. Logging it also makes the substitution visible in cooking history for free, and substitutionsMade already exists for exactly this.

Accessibility

  • The radial needs an explicit traversal order; a ring of absolutely-positioned nodes has no sensible default. Order by the engine's array order and set .accessibilitySortPriority, or the layout reads as scrambled.
  • Dietary badges must not be colour-only — keep the text (DAIRY-FREE, VEGAN).
  • If the radial cannot be made to traverse cleanly, ship the list on both size classes. A readable list beats an elegant graph nobody can navigate.
Deliberately not decided. Animation and transition curves for the expand, haptics, and whether the Sankey is tappable to filter steps. None of these block a first implementation, and all are better judged against a running build than specified on paper.
