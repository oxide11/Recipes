Creamy Cajun Chicken Pasta

Thin strips of chicken tossed in Cajun spice and seared in butter, then simmered in a quick cream sauce with green onion and sun-dried tomatoes. Poured over linguine and finished with Parmesan. One skillet, on the table in 25 minutes, and rich enough that a little goes a long way.

Method

hands-onwaiting
  1. 1
    Season the chicken
    4 min hands-on

    Cut 2 chicken breasts into thin strips.

    Toss with 2 tsp Cajun seasoning in a bowl.

  2. 2
    Cook the linguine
    1 min hands-on · 9 min wait10 min timer

    Boil 4 oz linguine until al dente.

  3. 3
    Sauté the chicken
    6 min hands-on6 min timer

    Melt 2 tbsp butter in a large skillet over medium heat.

    Sauté the chicken until tender, about 5 to 7 minutes.

  4. 4
    Make the sauce
    3 min hands-on

    Reduce the heat.

    Add 1 green onion, 1½ cups cream and 2 tbsp sun-dried tomatoes.

    Season with ¼ tsp basil, ¼ tsp salt, ⅛ tsp garlic powder and ⅛ tsp pepper. Heat through.

  5. 5
    Toss and serve
    2 min hands-on

    Pour over the hot linguine and toss with ¼ cup Parmesan.
