Hands-Free Cooking with Blink Navigation
Cooking Mode works with Apple Switch Control, so you can advance steps by blinking or with a head movement. Voice readback, auto-advancing timers and keep-screen-awake mean you never touch a screen with wet hands.
🧠
On-Device AI — Fully Private
Generate recipes and meal plans, estimate nutrition and infer steps with Apple Intelligence. Nothing leaves the device, and no API key is required. Cloud models are opt-in, never the default.
🥕
Zero-Waste Smart Matching
Cross-references your pantry against your library to surface what you can cook right now — with coverage percentages, missing-ingredient counts, and priority for whatever is about to expire.
⏱️
Live Activity Cooking Timers
Step timers run on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. Know when to flip, stir or pull from the oven without reopening the app.
🛒
Voice-Guided Smart Shopping
Lists built from your meal plan — merged, scaled, sorted by aisle and with pantry stock already subtracted. It reads each section aloud and waits for you to confirm, hands free.
📸
Receipt OCR & Spend Tracking
Photograph a grocery receipt and Vision OCR pulls out items, prices and totals, matches them to your list, and tracks spend against your weekly budget.
📊
Nutrition & Cooking Metrics
Daily and weekly calories and macros against your goals, plus a year-in-review: most-cooked cuisines, go-to ingredients, hours at the stove and estimated savings vs dining out.
📦
Barcode Pantry Scanning
Live camera barcode detection with automatic product lookup via Open Food Facts — category and nutrition filled in for you, with expiry tracking and restock suggestions from your own cooking history.
